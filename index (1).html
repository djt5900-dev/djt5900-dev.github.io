About

I'm an Electrical Engineering student at Penn State (Class of 2028) focused on RF and hardware design. As an engineering intern at Rodelco Electronics, I write VHDL firmware that drives a direct digital synthesizer to generate radar-style chirp, frequency-hopping, and phase-coded waveforms, and I characterize RF hardware on a vector network analyzer. Outside the lab I played Division I rugby for Penn State and I'm a member of IEEE.

Currently seeking Summer 2027 internships in defense, RF, and hardware engineering. U.S. citizen, eligible for security clearance.

FPGA-Controlled DDS Waveforms

VHDL on an Intel FPGA driving an AD9914 over SPI to produce linear chirps, frequency hopping, and Barker-coded phase modulation across 400–500 MHz. Tracked down a stray CFR2 register bit that locked the output to a fixed tone and validated the fix against the eval board.

Eight-ROM Waveform Generator

Converted a legacy schematic-based FPGA design into clean VHDL RTL: eight MIF-initialized ROMs behind a multiplexer, verified with self-checking ModelSim testbenches before going to hardware.
